We study supermassive black holes through large-scale numerical simulations and X-ray satellite data analysis. No prior experience in programming or physics is required; students interested in space and computing are welcome.

## What you will learn
- Programming (e.g. Python) and basic data analysis
- Numerical simulations on supercomputers
- Analysis of X-ray satellite observations
- How to read papers and present research
